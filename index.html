Hunt the Wumpus

How to Play

Welcome to the cave! Your goal is to find and shoot the fearsome Wumpus.

  • Hazards:
    • Bottomless Pits: You will feel a breeze nearby. Falling in is fatal.
    • Super Bats: You will hear flapping nearby. They will drop you in a random room.
    • The Wumpus: You will smell a terrible stench nearby. It will eat you!
  • Controls:
    • CTRL + ↑↓←→ to Move
    • ALT + ↑↓←→ to Shoot (Costs 1 Arrow)

You have 5 arrows. If you miss, the Wumpus might move. Good luck!
